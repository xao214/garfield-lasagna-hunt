Garfield's Lasagna Hunt

Six levels between Garfield and the biggest lasagna in town. A personal, non-commercial fan game made for Andy.

Turn your iPad sideways (landscape) for the best view.
D-pad ◀ ▶Walk (stairs are automatic)
B or AJump (hold to jump higher)
Y or XPounce: a dash that stuns enemies
▼ + jumpDrop through a shelf, table or branch
STARTStart · pause · confirm · continue after Game Over
SELECTEnter a password (on the title screen)

Keyboard: arrows, X = B, Z = A, S = Y, A = X, Enter = Start, V = Select. Bluetooth game controllers work too.
Tip: in Safari use Share → Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app. No sound? Check the iPad volume.
